Assumptions and replay

One-period Gaussian factors, deterministic scenario LGD and EAD, fixed marginal PDs. No rating migration, dynamic recoveries or empirical macro calibration. This is a research prototype, not a regulatory capital calculation.

stressatlas verify --out demo validates artifact hashes and reruns all paths from saved inputs and seed. The CSV sample contains the first 200 paths, not the complete tail.
